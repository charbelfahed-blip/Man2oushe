
# 🫓 Man2oushe Lab 🇱🇧

### Authentic Lebanese Man’oushe — Interactive Recipe App

Welcome to **Man2oushe Lab**, an interactive recipe calculator and step-by-step baking guide for authentic Lebanese manakish.

Designed for the **Ooni Karu 2 Pro**, with overnight cold fermentation and flexible baking options.

## 🚀 Launch the App

[🫓 Open Man2oushe Lab](./Manoushe_Lab_Guided.html)

## ✨ Features

- 🧮 Adjustable number of manakish (1–30)
- 📏 Adjustable diameter (18–30 cm)
- ⚖️ Automatic dough-ball weight calculations
- 💧 Automatic ingredient scaling
- 🌀 KitchenAid or manual mixing instructions
- ❄️ Overnight cold fermentation
- 🕒 Customizable preparation schedule
- 🔥 Ooni Karu 2 Pro baking instructions
- 👨‍🍳 Interactive step-by-step cooking mode
- ✅ Progress tracking and checklists
- ⏱️ Cooking timers
- 📱 Mobile-friendly interface

## 🫓 Default Recipe

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Quantity | 8 manakish |
| Diameter | 24 cm |
| Dough weight | ~149 g |
| Hydration | 61% |
| Fermentation | Overnight |
| Mixing | KitchenAid |
| Oven | Ooni Karu 2 Pro |

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| All-purpose flour | 490 g |
| Caputo 00 flour | 175 g |
| Whole-wheat flour | 35 g |
| Water | 427 g |
| Fine salt | 14 g |
| Sugar | 14 g |
| Olive oil | 21 g |
| Instant yeast | 2.5 g |

### Za'atar Topping

- 70 g Lebanese za'atar
- 90 g extra-virgin olive oil

## 👨‍🍳 How to Use

1. Open the interactive app.
2. Choose the number of manakish.
3. Select the desired diameter.
4. Choose KitchenAid or hand mixing.
5. Review automatically calculated ingredients.
6. Press **Start Guided Bake**.
7. Follow each preparation step.
8. Track your progress and baking results.

## 🔥 Baking Guide

**Oven:** Ooni Karu 2 Pro

**Stone temperature:** Approximately 350–380°C

**Baking time:** Approximately 1–2.5 minutes

Adjust the flame and rotate frequently for even baking.
